## About OSTERA

OSTERA, The Open Standards and Technology Engineering and Research Association, is a modern, global, nonprofit, member-driven standards organization that is designed for speed, agility, and impact. We are reimagining how international standards are built: faster, fairer, and open to all. OSTERA is registered in the Canton of Geneva, Switzerland.

Participation is open to OSTERA members. However, anyone may comment on a draft during its Public Review. Read the [Contributing Guidelines](https://github.com/OSTERA-org-Working-Groups/.github/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) before you open an issue or a pull request.

## Becoming a Member

To join a Working Group, your organization (or you, as an individual) **MUST** first become a member of OSTERA. See [Membership](https://ostera.org/membership/) for further information, or contact [membership@ostera.org](mailto:membership@ostera.org).

## Joining a Working Group

Once you are a member, you can join the Working Groups you are interested in through the [OSTERA Portal](https://member.ostera.org/), or you can send a request to the Secretariat at [secretariat@ostera.org](mailto:secretariat@ostera.org).

## Commenting on a Draft

Anyone may comment on a draft during its Public Review, even if they are not a member of OSTERA. Every open Public Review is listed on the [Public Reviews]({{ '/reviews/' | relative_url }}) page. To make a comment, click on the document that you are interested in from the Draft column on that page. Then from the document's details page, find the How to Comment section, which will contain a link to the Working Group's repository for opening an issue and the link for sending an email to [public-review@ostera.org](mailto:public-review@ostera.org).

## Working Group Repositories

Each Working Group has a public repository in the [OSTERA Working Groups](https://github.com/OSTERA-org-Working-Groups/) GitHub organization, although not every Working Group uses GitHub for its development. Before you open an issue or a pull request, please read the [Contributing Guidelines](https://github.com/OSTERA-org-Working-Groups/.github/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Public Repositories

These repositories are public. Materials that are confidential to OSTERA members **MUST NOT** be stored in them, or anywhere else on GitHub. They **MUST** instead be stored in the [OSTERA Portal](https://member.ostera.org/).

## Publications

Approved documents are published at [pub.ostera.org](https://pub.ostera.org/), not in the Working Group repositories.

## Policies

The official OSTERA policies, including the IPR and Copyright Policy, the Working Methods, and the Code of Conduct, are published at [ostera.org/policies/](https://ostera.org/policies/).

## Intellectual Property

Anything you submit to OSTERA, such as an issue, a pull request, a comment, or an email, is a contribution under the [OSTERA IPR and Copyright Policy](https://ostera.org/policies/), and by submitting it you agree to that policy. Do not submit anything you do not have the right to contribute.

If you have knowledge of intellectual property rights, including patents, copyrights, trademarks, and service marks, for software or text, the use of which may be required to implement or publish any of the work items being considered, please disclose it by following the instructions at [ostera.org/ipr/](https://ostera.org/ipr/) or contact the Secretariat at [secretariat@ostera.org](mailto:secretariat@ostera.org).

## Code of Conduct

All participants are expected to follow the OSTERA [Code of Conduct](https://github.com/OSTERA-org-Working-Groups/.github/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

## Questions

For general questions about OSTERA, visit [OSTERA.org](https://ostera.org/) or contact the Secretariat at [secretariat@ostera.org](mailto:secretariat@ostera.org).

## Becoming a Member

To join a working group, your organization (or you, as an individual) must first be an OSTERA member. See [Membership](https://ostera.org/membership) for the membership levels, or contact us at [info@ostera.org](mailto:info@ostera.org).

## Joining a Working Group

Once you are a member, contact us at [info@ostera.org](mailto:info@ostera.org) and tell us which working groups you would like to join.

## Working Group Repositories

Each working group has a public repository in the [OSTERA Working Groups](https://github.com/OSTERA-org-Working-Groups) GitHub organization. Every repository uses the same layout:

| Folder | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| 01-Governance | Charter, scope statement, and working group procedures |
| 02-Meetings | Agendas and minutes |
| 03-Work-Items | Source files for drafts in progress |
| 04-Liaisons | Public liaison statements |
| 05-Marketing-Materials | Presentations and promotional material |
| 06-Website | Source for the working group website |

These repositories are public. Material that is confidential to OSTERA members or to a liaison partner must not be placed in them.

## Contributing

Before contributing, please read the [Contributing Guidelines](https://github.com/OSTERA-org-Working-Groups/.github/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) and the [Code of Conduct](https://github.com/OSTERA-org-Working-Groups/.github/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

By submitting any contribution (an issue, a pull request, or a comment), you agree that your contribution is made under the [OSTERA IPR and Copyright Policy](https://ostera.org/policies/). Do not submit material that you do not have the right to contribute.

## Published Documents

Drafts in working group repositories are works in progress. They have not been approved by the Association and must not be cited as OSTERA publications. Approved documents are published at [pub.ostera.org](https://pub.ostera.org/).
